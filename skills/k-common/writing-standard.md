# Writing standard

This team writes skills and docs to ASD-STE100 (Issue 9, January 2025).
The official source is https://www.asd-ste100.org.
ASD owns the specification and the approved dictionary.
Do not copy their content into this repo.
When a word or a rule is in doubt, check the official specification.

## Sentences

- Keep a procedural sentence to 20 words or fewer.
- Keep a descriptive sentence to 25 words or fewer.
- Split a long sentence into two sentences. Do not delete information to make it fit.
- Give one instruction in each sentence.
- Do not use semicolons.
- Use a plain hyphen for every dash. Do not use an en dash or an em dash.

## Paragraphs and lists

- Keep each paragraph to one topic and about six sentences or fewer.
- Use lists for steps, conditions, and options.

## Words and grammar

- Use the active voice. Use the passive voice only in descriptive text, when the actor is unknown or does not matter.
- Use the imperative, simple present, simple past, and simple future.
- Use one word for one meaning. Use the same word for the same thing every time.
- Use a plain word instead of a rare or figurative word. Avoid idioms and phrasal verbs.
- Use a verb for an action. Do not use a noun made from the verb.
- Keep a noun string to three words or fewer.
- Technical names and terms are allowed when the reader needs them. Use a term only in its real technical meaning.

## Brevity

Cut filler and split sentences to be short.
Do not omit articles, verbs, or subjects.
Do not write fragments.
Write "Fix the race condition in the queue worker."
Do not write "Race condition fixed, queue worker."
